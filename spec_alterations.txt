Spec Alterations
================

1. Graph Connectivity (implemented)
-----------------------------------
Problem: Walk graph has disconnected components causing zero-score anomalies.
Solution: Filter graph to largest connected component before snapping POIs and grid points.

2. Kernel Decay Rate (proposed)
-------------------------------
Problem: Exponential kernel with lambda=300m decays too slowly, causing high scores
to "bleed" into areas like the Charles River where walking access is limited.

Current: lambda=300m gives 19% weight at 500m, 3.6% at 1000m
Proposed: lambda=150m gives 3.6% weight at 500m, 0.1% at 1000m

This creates sharper contrast between areas with nearby POIs vs distant ones.

Alternative: Use power_law kernel with p=2.5 or p=3 for even steeper falloff.
