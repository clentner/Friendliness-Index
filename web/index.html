How to read this map

One contribution per mapped destination, fading with walking distance. A fixed scale keeps colors comparable as you pan. Clear areas are outside coverage or too far from the walking network—not a low score.

This is an amenity-proximity experiment, not a street-safety rating. Mapped locations stand in for entrances; barriers and missing map data can affect results.
